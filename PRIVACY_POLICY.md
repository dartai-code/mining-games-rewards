# Privacy Policy - Dart AI Miner

**Last Updated**: September 25, 2025

## Overview

Dart AI Miner ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, game scores, mining session data
- **Ad Interaction Data**: Ad views, clicks, and completion status (collected by AdMob)

### Information You Provide
- **Referral Codes**: Unique codes generated for sharing
- **Social Media Interactions**: When you complete social tasks (external links only)

## How We Use Your Information

### Core App Functionality
- Track mining sessions and virtual points
- Calculate game rewards and maintain high scores
- Process referral bonuses and task completions
- Maintain wallet balance and transaction history


### Advertising
- Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
- Provide rewarded video ads for bonus features
- Measure ad effectiveness and user engagement

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
- All game progress, balances, and transaction history stored locally on your device
- No personal data transmitted to our servers
- Data persists until app is uninstalled or cache is cleared

### Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob**: Collects advertising data according to Google's Privacy Policy
- **Social Media Platforms**: External links to Twitter, YouTube, Telegram (their privacy policies apply)

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except:
- **AdMob Integration**: Anonymous usage data for ad serving
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by law or legal process

## In-App Currency

### DART Coins
- Virtual, non-transferable in-app currency
- No real-world monetary value
- Cannot be exchanged for cash, gift cards, or cryptocurrency
- Used solely for app engagement and gamification

### Important Disclaimer
DART coins are entertainment-only virtual currency with no cash value. We make no promises or guarantees regarding:
- Conversion to real money
- Exchange for goods or services
- Transfer between users or devices

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

## Advertising and Children
- We use Google AdMob's family-friendly ad serving
- No targeted advertising based on personal information
- Ads are general audience appropriate

## Your Rights

### Data Control
- View your local data through the app's wallet and transaction history
- Delete all data by uninstalling the app
- Clear specific data through app settings

### Opt-Out Options

- No transmission of sensitive personal information
